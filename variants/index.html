Day 002 / 100 Days of Digital Craft

One mission hero, three directions

Three fictional NASA mission landing-page heroes, each built around a real-time vgpu / WebGPU render with a static fallback. Use a WebGPU browser (recent Chrome or Edge) for the live versions.

  1. V1 · Event Horizon /

    An observation deck aboard the vessel Horizon. Observe, scan and record a black hole, with passing debris, a relay probe and opt-in sound.

    Living observation deck

  2. V2 · Ardent /v2/

    A crewed-cargo Mars mission organised around the launch window. Stardust forms the mission patch.

    In progress: particles done; Mars, intro and send-your-name next

  3. V3 · Dock /v3/

    Thread the needle: drag the ring station into alignment, hold, and the station (and the page) comes online.

    Complete motion pass
