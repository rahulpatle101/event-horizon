Vessel Horizon · Mission log

Expedition log

Watch the edge. Never cross it.

Horizon holds station far outside a black hole's event horizon. Nothing that crosses it can signal back, so every observation comes from light that escaped.

01Objective

Map the shadow and the thin photon ring around it to test general relativity where gravity is strongest.

02The vessel

Horizon is a fictional deep-space science vessel with an observation deck, an optical imager, an X-ray timing array, and a laser link home. It never dives inward.

03Observation plan

  1. Capture
    Slow capture into a wide orbit.
  2. Survey
    Image the disk and its Doppler-brightened side.
  3. Ring campaign
    Step inward to 13.5 Rs; time echoes in the photon ring.
  4. Relay
    Stream observations home by laser.
